Why families like it

Everything gift-giving needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Built for the way families actually shop for each other — birthdays, holidays, and everything in between.

Spoiler-proof by design

The person you're shopping for never sees who claimed or bought their gifts. You decide, gift by gift, what they get to see.

Claim it, so nobody doubles up

Mark a gift as yours and the rest of the family sees it's covered. Ordered, purchased, wrapped — track it all in a simple shopping checklist.

Add gifts from anywhere

Share from any shopping app on Android, drag a link on your computer, use the browser bookmarklet, or import a whole Amazon wish list at once.

Private gifts & groups

Planning something with just a few people? Keep gifts private, or use Privacy Groups so only the right relatives see them.

No accounts, no cloud

Your family's devices sync directly with each other over encrypted connections. There's no sign-up and no company server holding your lists.

Occasions & birthdays

Organize ideas by occasion, keep track of birthdays, and give something to the whole family with a shared “Family” recipient.

The surprise stays a surprise

Everyone sees exactly what they should.

  • Claims and purchases are hidden from the recipient. Not just tucked away — the app never shows them.
  • You control what's visible. A gift idea someone adds for you stays secret unless its creator chooses to show it.
  • The rest of the family stays in sync. Everyone else sees what's claimed, so presents don't overlap.

Up and running in a few minutes

Privacy first

Your data stays on your family's devices

Family Gifts has no cloud database, no user accounts, and no ads.

100% local & peer-to-peer

All gift ideas, photos, prices, notes, and family member names live only on your family's devices and sync directly over end-to-end encrypted connections.

Optional, anonymous stats

To know which platforms need the most attention, the app can send only anonymous aggregate counts during update checks: short one-way hashed group & device tags, device platform, app version, and family member count.

Never collected, easy to turn off

We never collect names, gift data, photos, invite keys, or IP addresses — and you can turn off anonymous update stats anytime in Family & Settings while still receiving updates.

What's new

    Setup

    Quick setup guide

    Every family creates its own private peer-to-peer vault right on their device — no cloud accounts or passwords required.

    Android installation & updates
    1. Tap Download on the Android APK above and open the finished file.
    2. When Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser (one-time prompt because Family Gifts is distributed directly, outside Google Play).
    3. Open Family Gifts and either tap Create Family Vault (to start your own family group) or return to your invite link to join an existing family.
    4. Updating later: download the new APK and tap Update — your gifts and family stay intact.
    Windows PC installation
    1. Download and double-click the Windows .exe installer.
    2. If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway (standard warning for indie apps without a commercial signing certificate).
    3. Open Family Gifts to start a new family vault or paste/open your family invite.
    Linux installation (.deb or .AppImage)
    • Ubuntu / Debian (.deb): open with Software Install, or run sudo apt install ./Family-Gifts-*.deb.
    • Universal (.AppImage): make it executable (chmod +x Family-Gifts-*.AppImage) and run it directly.
    How family syncing & spoiler protection work
    • Built-in spoiler protection: gifts added for you are automatically hidden from you (or have buyer/claim details masked if marked visible).
    • Peer-to-peer sync: devices sync directly over encrypted connections. Leaving one PC open (or enabling Anchor Mode on Wi-Fi in Family & Settings) helps family members sync smoothly even if they aren't online at the exact same moment.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does the person I'm shopping for see what I bought them?

    No. When a gift is on your own list, claims, assignments, and purchase status are hidden from you, and you can't edit or claim gifts other people added for you. Whoever adds a gift decides whether it's visible to the recipient at all.

    How do we avoid buying the same gift twice?

    Claim a gift and everyone else in the family sees it's taken. Marking a gift Ordered, Purchased, or Wrapped claims it for you automatically if nobody has yet, and the My Shopping list keeps your own checklist in one place.

    Do I need an account or an internet service to use it?

    There are no accounts or passwords. Your family's vault lives on your own devices, which sync directly with each other over encrypted connections. If devices aren't online at the same time, leaving one PC open or turning on Anchor Mode helps changes catch up.

    How do I add family members?

    Create an invite from within the app and send it by QR code, email, or text. The link opens this page, helps them install the app, and then joins them to your family automatically.

    How do I add a gift from a store website?

    On Android, use the Share menu in your browser or shopping app and choose Family Gifts. On a computer, drag the product link into the app or use the Add-to-Family-Gifts bookmarklet. You can also add several links at once, import an Amazon wish list, or paste a photo or screenshot.

    Why does Windows or Android warn me when I install?

    Family Gifts is distributed directly rather than through an app store. Android asks you to allow “Install unknown apps” once for your browser, and Windows SmartScreen shows a warning for apps without a commercial signing certificate. The setup guide above walks through both prompts.

    Is Family Gifts free?

    Family Gifts is shared with friends and family at no charge, and it contains no ads.

    Is there an iPhone or Mac version?

    Not yet. Family Gifts currently runs on Android, Windows, and Linux.

    Why isn't it on Google Play?

    Family Gifts is currently distributed directly while it's shared with friends and family, so you install the Android app from a file instead of an app store.

    What information does Family Gifts collect?

    Your gifts, photos, and family details stay on your devices. The app can optionally send anonymous aggregate counts during update checks (hashed tags, platform, app version, and family size). It never collects names, gift data, photos, invite keys, or IP addresses, and you can turn the stats off in Family & Settings.
